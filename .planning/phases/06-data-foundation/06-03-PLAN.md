<objective>
Enforce DATA-03's `isTest: false` read filter on the two remaining public business-display
surfaces this project has: the paginated `/directory` index, and the `/business/[slug]` detail
page plus its `reviews`/`photos`/`questions` API sub-routes and the collections add-item route.

Purpose: These are the two entry points where a guest reaches a SPECIFIC business (by browsing
the directory, or by direct/shared URL) — DATA-03 requires a test fixture to be unreachable here
even if someone knows or guesses its slug.
Output: /directory and /business/[slug] (plus its sub-routes) never expose a test fixture; a
test-fixture slug 404s exactly like a nonexistent one.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Guest/anonymous browser → /directory, /business/[slug] | Public, unauthenticated read surfaces reachable by direct URL, not just in-app navigation |
| Logged-in user → reviews/photos/questions/collections sub-routes | Authenticated write-adjacent paths that still resolve a business by slug/id from client-supplied route params |
